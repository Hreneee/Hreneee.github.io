# Figma Make / Gemini Prompt Brief

## Goal

Explore section composition, world layout, and content framing for a fully static
portfolio that feels like a living computational garden: dream-like, elegant,
research-forward, and game-like without becoming noisy or childish.

## Generate These Outputs

1. Homepage gateway concepts
2. Research zone composition concepts
3. Projects zone composition concepts
4. Experience zone composition concepts
5. About and contact sanctuary concepts
6. Recruiter-friendly overlay navigation concepts
7. Mobile-friendly reinterpretations of the world

## Prompt: Homepage World Gateway

```text
Design a cinematic homepage composition for a personal portfolio website set in a living computational garden. The world should feel dream-like, immersive, elegant, pristine, and emotionally memorable. It should signal research, AI/ML, systems thinking, and design. The scene should include a stylized female avatar as the central presence, calm bioluminescent flora, subtle architectural structures, reflective surfaces, and a small luminous companion animal. The composition should feel high-end and intentional, not childish, noisy, fantasy-chaotic, or game UI-heavy. Include clear space for a professional navigation layer and brief introductory copy.
```

## Prompt: Research Zone

```text
Design a 3D-inspired section composition for a research zone inside a living computational garden portfolio. The space should feel like a greenhouse observatory for inquiry and pattern recognition. Use luminous botanical forms, intelligent structures, signal-like details, reflective pools, and restrained NPC presences that suggest observation and experimentation. Keep the layout elegant and readable with room for research summaries, publication cards, and concise methodology callouts.
```

## Prompt: Projects Zone

```text
Design a section composition for a project-build zone inside an immersive computational garden portfolio. The mood should be serene and intelligent, with sculptural worktables, modular structures, signal pathways, data-flora, and a sense of systems assembly. This should support project cards, tool stacks, and build-story overlays while preserving a dream-like world atmosphere.
```

## Prompt: Experience Zone

```text
Design a section composition for an experience zone representing operational thinking, coordination, and care within a living computational garden. Use pathways, irrigation-like systems, structured movement, and quiet supporting figures to suggest process design, healthcare workflows, and institutional systems. Preserve a premium, calm, editorial look.
```

## Prompt: About / Contact Sanctuary

```text
Design an intimate inner-garden sanctuary for the about and contact sections of a portfolio. The space should feel reflective, poised, and personal, with a stylized female avatar present as a calm host. Use soft bioluminescent plants, architectural restraint, and room for concise personal narrative, resume access, and contact actions.
```

## Export Formats

- PNG for still compositions
- PDF for presentation boards
- SVG only for simple diagrams or interface motifs
- short text summaries describing why each concept works

## Good Enough Before Integration

- strong composition
- clear hierarchy
- consistent mood
- believable placement of content overlays

Pixel-perfect UI polish is not required here because the site will be rebuilt in code.
